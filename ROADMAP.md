# Design Notes

## Inspiration
- [Trouble In Mineville Wiki (KIG network)](https://kignetwork.fandom.com/wiki/Trouble_in_Mineville_(TIMV))
    - Originally the TIMV minigame was on the old Hive network for Java edition.
- [Murder Mystery Wiki (Hive network)](https://playhive.fandom.com/wiki/Murder_Mystery)
    - After Hive made the switch to Bedrock edition, they reworked TIMV into a simpler Murder Mystery minigame.
- [Murder Mystery Wiki (Hypixel network)](https://hypixel.fandom.com/wiki/Murder_Mystery)
    - Hypixel's Murder Mystery is probably the most well known version of this minigame.
- [Murder Mystery Minigame Plugin (SpigotMC)](https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/murder-mystery-1-8-8-26-1.66614/)
    - A relatively old, but still maintained plugin that adds a Hypixel-like Murder Mystery minigame.
## Roles

### Innocent
- has to eat and sleep
- 

### Detective
- better firearm handling
- infinite firearm ammo (with cooldown)

### Murderer
- can kill anyone
- can poison food
- can trap beds

# MVP

- Game Time Limit
- End Round after conditions are met:
  - Time runs out
  - Only murderers are alive
  - All murderers die
- Nearby Only Chat

## Achievements
- A Record of Disaster (As a Detective, find and investigate a dead body.)
- Last Man Standing (Be the last one alive as any role except the Murderer.)

- The Slaughter Begins (Kill a player as a Murderer.)
- Next on the Chopping Block (Kill 4 players in one round as a Murderer.)
- Workin' Overtime (Kill 6 players in one round as a Murderer.)
- Black and White with Red All Over (Kill 8 players in one round as a Murderer.)

- Close Call (Escape a Murderer's attack.)
- Blood Work

- No Training Needed (As an Innocent, use a Detective's weapon to kill a Murderer.)
- Hide & Seek Master (As an Innocent, survive until the clock runs out.)
