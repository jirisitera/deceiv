# Deceiv
A modern take on murder mystery minigame genre.
## Inspiration
- [Trouble In Mineville Wiki (KIG network)](https://kignetwork.fandom.com/wiki/Trouble_in_Mineville_(TIMV))
  - Originally the TIMV minigame was on the old Hive network for Java edition.
- [Murder Mystery Wiki (Hive network)](https://playhive.fandom.com/wiki/Murder_Mystery)
  - After Hive made the switch to Bedrock edition, they reworked TIMV into a simpler Murder Mystery minigame.
- [Murder Mystery Wiki (Hypixel network)](https://hypixel.fandom.com/wiki/Murder_Mystery)
  - Hypixel's Murder Mystery is probably the most well known version of this minigame.
- [Murder Mystery Minigame Plugin (SpigotMC)](https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/murder-mystery-1-8-8-26-1.66614/)
  - A relatively old, but still maintained plugin that adds a Hypixel-like Murder Mystery minigame.
